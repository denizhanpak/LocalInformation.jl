"Higher-order" is TotalCorrelation, DualTotalCorrelation, OInformation and SInformation. Anything else throws an ArgumentError that names the estimator and lists the supported ones. Bias-corrected entropy estimators (MillerMadow, ChaoShen, Jackknife, Schuermann, ...) are not supported: their estimates are not sample means of per-sample terms.

Notes:

  • Discrete local entropies are -log(n(ω_t)/N) from the counts of the joint outcomes, with exactly the encoding Associations uses. With an embedding outcome space (e.g. OrdinalPatterns) there is one local value per encoded symbol.
  • Gaussian local entropies are ½(d log 2π + log det Σ + N/(N-1) z'z), with Σ the N - 1-normalised covariance that Associations uses and z = L \ (x - μ). The factor N/(N-1) makes the mean exactly Associations' closed form. This equals -log 𝒩(x; μ, Σ̂) + (d/2) log(N/(N-1)) with the maximum-likelihood covariance Σ̂; the constant cancels in every mutual information and higher-order measure.
  • k-NN local entropies are the per-sample terms of ComplexityMeasures' Kraskov (-ψ(k) + ψ(N) + log V_D + D log ρ_t) and KozachenkoLeonenko, with the same Euclidean k-d tree search and Theiler window.
  • KSG1 / KSG2 / FPVP local values are the per-sample terms of Associations' estimators, e.g. ψ(k) + ψ(N) - ψ(n_x(t)) - ψ(n_y(t)) for KSG1, with Associations' conventions: Chebyshev joint k-th neighbour outside the Theiler window, marginal counts ≤ ε_t including the point itself and without Theiler window. (Kraskov et al. count strictly inside ε, as does syntropy; the difference is exactly accounted for in the tests.)
